### Changed
- **Booking rows say who and what, not how** (roadmap 43). A row's icon now names the counterparty: money out, money in, or for a transfer between your own accounts the other account (Household's home, Savings' anchor). A small chip after the name says "Pending" for a booking not executed yet, or how often a standing order runs ("Monthly"), also on each executed payment of one; a one-off payment has none. The repeat icon no longer marks recurring rows. On All bookings and Account details, for the built-in bookings and the ones you make. Recurring payments made in the app are monthly for now. The Chip component gains a tonal variant for this.
- **Built-in bookings open with their real status** (roadmap 43). Pending and recurring ones on All bookings read "Executed" in their sheet; now they read "Pending". Rent no longer opens as a transfer between accounts. Built-in bookings show no Edit until an edit of one can last past a reload (roadmap 42).
- Spec: `specs/2026-11-05-booking-chips/`.
- **All bookings: month separators** (roadmap 24). Each month in the list starts with a band naming it ("JUNE"; with the year when a tab crosses one, "JANUARY 2027"), in all three tabs and for payments made in the app too. Each band is a heading, so a screen reader can jump month to month. A month chip scrolls to its band, just under the docked chips, and while scrolling the chip of the month in view becomes active; a chip's own scroll doesn't flick through the months on the way. Search hides the band of a month with no match. With reduced motion the chip jumps instead of scrolling.
- **Focus stays on the month chip.** Enter or a click on a chip used to drop focus to the page when the chips docked; now it moves to the same month's chip in the docked bar, and back when the bar undocks.
- **Pending and Recurring stay in date order.** A payment made in the app now goes in at its date among the others, not above them all.
- Spec: `specs/2026-11-04-month-separators/`.
- **"Reset demo data" asks first and is never disabled** (roadmap 22). Profile's button opens "Reset demo data?" (Cancel or Reset; Escape keeps the data) and, after a reset, a line under it says "Demo data reset." for as long as the page is open, read out by screen readers. The button no longer greys out for a moment or swaps its label. The section's text now says what a reset clears: payments, trades and investments, with the starting balances restored.
- Spec: `specs/2026-11-04-reset-demo-confirm/`.

### Fixed
- **Every booking's sheet offers what you can do with it** (roadmap 42). The built-in pending payments (Swisscom, Helvetia Insurance and Tax office Zurich on All bookings, Apple on Account details) showed no actions; now a pending payment has Edit, Delete and Copy, an executed payment out has Copy, and a pending transfer made in the app has Delete. Standing orders keep what they had until roadmap 47; trades and money in have none.
- **An edited or deleted example row stays gone after a reload.** It used to come back next to its edited copy. Profile's "Reset demo data" brings the rows back. A date group or month left with no row goes with it, and an empty Pending tab says "No pending payments."
- **Delete asks first** ("Delete payment?" or "Delete transfer?", Escape keeps it), then removes the booking; a transfer loses both legs. Screen readers hear "Payment deleted." Apple's CHF 100 counts in the Household balance only while Apple is listed, so deleting or editing it no longer counts it twice.
- **Copy** opens Pay with the recipient and amount, as soon as possible; Execute adds a new payment and the original stays.
- **The sheet's footer reads "Done"**, not "Confirm", on every booking.
- Spec: `specs/2026-11-05-booking-actions/`.
- **Confirmation dialogs keep focus and can't confirm twice** (roadmap 41). After Buy, Sell, Pay or a transfer, Tab could reach the sheet behind the "done" dialog, its Confirm included. Now the sheet is inert while the dialog shows and focus is on Done (with the ring only after a keyboard action); Done hands focus back to the button that opened the flow. Browser Back from the dialog closed it and left Pay or the transfer live on its last step, so a second payment was possible; Back now closes the flow with no prompt, and one booking or order is recorded.
- **One layout and one plain sentence for every confirmation** (roadmap 41). Pay, the transfer, Buy and Sell now show the same dialog: a sentence ("Your payment to Hans Meyer is done.", "Your order to buy 12 Toyota Motor Corporation shares has been placed."), the amount with "CHF" small before it on one baseline, and for a trade one line with its terms. The trade's empty "to" row is gone, "pcs." reads "share" or "shares", and long names wrap. Screen readers hear the sentence as the dialog's name.

Both legs, on the debit and the credit account's list.

### Added
- **Pick the execution date** (roadmap 28). With "As soon as possible" (Pay) or "Immediately" (the transfer) off, "Execute on" now opens a calendar: a pop-up under the field on desktop, a bottom sheet on a phone. Any working day from the next one to twelve months ahead can be picked; today has a ring, weekends and days out of range are dimmed. Turning the toggle off sets the next working day, not today, and turning it on goes back to today. The picked date is when a later payment or transfer runs and when a recurring one or a standing order starts; it shows in the summary and the confirmation and orders the Pending tab. It works by keyboard (arrows, Page Up and Page Down, Home and End, Enter; focus goes back to the field). New `js/date-picker.js`.
- Spec: `specs/2026-11-04-execution-date/`.
- **Fund the asset management mandate** (roadmap 18). Each opened mandate is a credit account in the internal transfer, after Household and Savings, with its strategy and balance; picking one puts Household as the debit account, and Savings can be picked instead. An executed transfer turns the Overview row from "Pending: CHF …" into the funded balance, which the Accounts & investment total counts; any amount, any number of times. Pending and recurring transfers fund nothing. The opening's completion says "Next: transfer {amount} to your asset management." and offers "Transfer now" above Done: in the Overview sheet it closes the sheet and opens the transfer prefilled (Household, the mandate, the opening's amount, Immediately), on its own page it goes to `overview.html#iat/fund/<id>`. A mandate transfer's booking details name the mandate.
- **Custody account is no longer a transfer target.** Its balance is fixed, so money sent there was lost; it stays the trade's account.
- The payment state's mandates gain `funded` (a number, 0 when missing); no new key, no schema change.
- Spec: `specs/2026-11-04-fund-mandate/`.
- **The assistant notices when a customer seems stuck** (roadmap 9f). Time on a step against its reading time, changing an answer again and again, coming back to a step, opening the same explanation twice or leaving the amount empty make the assistant offer that step's own question, once, quietly: on desktop the invitation shows the question with "Show me" and "No, thanks", on a phone a bubble rises above Help?. Newbie gets it on every step, All-rounder only on knowledge, amount, risk, tax and documents and later, Power user never. The third nudge, and any on documents or the summary, also offers a person. Nudges are remembered with the progress for the session. `?nudge=fast` shortens the waits for testing.
- **Talk to an advisor, as a handoff.** It offers a callback or a video meeting, three times on working days within office hours, and a confirmation; the booking is kept with the progress and can be changed or cancelled from any step. The exit screen's "Book an appointment" uses it. Nothing is asked or sent.
- **Components:** the nudge and the handoff in their own sections.
- **The phone's question layer slides down when it closes**, mirroring its slide-up, with the flow sheets' timing (up 0.35 s, down 0.28 s); focus returns to Help? once it is gone. With reduced motion it closes at once.
