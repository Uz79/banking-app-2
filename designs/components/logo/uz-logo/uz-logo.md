# UZ logo

Circular UZ mark for the sidebar.

## Variants

| Variant | Path | Description |
|---------|------|-------------|
| `circle` | `variants/circle/default.svg` | 24px disc with the UZ lettering cut out |

## States

- `default` — only state exported

## Notes

- Used as the sidebar logo mark, beside the word Bank when the rail is extended.
- In the app the mark is the `i-uz-logo` icon, so it follows the current text color.
