# Fridge advisor (8a, the flow): requirements

Branch: `feature/fridge-advisor`

Roadmap item 8a (`specs/roadmap.md`). "How much should I invest?": the first step of opening an investment product, told as a fridge. Based on a flow Klaudiusz designed with a bank; reference frames in `~/Documents/Projects/AI Portfolio Projects/Banking App/_resources/261022_fridge-food/scenario` (six frames, in German; the copy here is English). The avatar acting (8b) and what follows "Show suitable products" (9) are separate items.

## Scope

**In:**

1. Entry from Overview > Offers > "Investment", which opens the flow as a sheet like the payment and trade flows, with Back and a close button. The item stops linking to `investment-product-details.html`
2. A 3D fridge in the theme colours (Kenney Furniture Kit `kitchenFridge.glb`) with the persona character standing next to it, idle only
3. Five beats: the closed fridge intro; a camera tour of each compartment with a short explanation; filling the main compartment; filling the reserve drawers; the door closes, a short cooling moment, the freezer opens with the investable amount
4. Sliders as the input (exact CHF value shown); food models on the shelves and in the drawers in proportion to the values, as a picture of them, not as the input
5. The result: the one-time investable amount, the yearly amount, a one-line note, and "Show suitable products", which opens a closing screen inside the flow (D6)
6. Reduced motion: no camera flights, still frames per compartment
7. About 15 food models and the fridge copied into `apps/web/assets/3d/fridge/`

**Out (not in this branch):**

- The avatar acting: opening doors, carrying items, cheering (8b)
- Product choice, risk profile, signing (item 9)
- Remembering entered values (D8: no storage key)
- Real accounts: the flow never reads or writes `uzBankWebPaymentState`
- Sound

**Agreed before this spec, confirmed and not re-opened:** the entry; the sheet; English copy; the Kenney fridge, with doors as separate nodes; the five beats; sliders as input, food as visualisation; the result formula's shape and the yearly amount (the formula is flagged for confirmation, D9); the one-line note; reduced motion as still frames; copy only the models used; reuse `createFigure` from `js/profile-persona-character.js`, no fork.

## Context

- Overview's Offers card is `overview.html` lines 2579 to 2616; the Investment item at 2602 is `<a class="list-item" href="investment-product-details.html">`. Accounts and Cards in the same card are not links.
- The Playground sheet pattern: `js/playground-sheet.js` (opener), `playground.html` (page), `js/playground.js` (page script, closes via `postMessage`), `.modal-overlay--playground` and `.playground-frame` in `css/styles.css`. The exit dialog is `js/payment-exit-confirm.js` with `js/dialog-focus.js`.
- `js/profile-persona-character.js` exports `createFigure`, `readTheme`, `monochromeLut`, `preparePalette`, `cssColor`, `THREE`. `GLTFLoader` is imported in `js/playground-world.js` from jsDelivr.
- Fridge measurements from the GLB: body 0.43 wide, 0.92 tall, 0.26 deep; `doorFreezer` at y 0.70 to 0.89, `doorFridge` at y 0.09 to 0.71; both doors have their hinge at x = 0.027.
- Food kit models share one texture, `colormap.png`, so one recoloured material covers all of them.
- Reference frames (German) show the freezer on top and the main compartment below, which matches the kit's fridge.
