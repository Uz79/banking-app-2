# The fridge as a film (8d): requirements

Branch: `feature/fridge-film`

Roadmap item 8d (`specs/roadmap.md`). The fridge advisor (`specs/2026-10-22-fridge-advisor/`) today splits its wide sheet into two columns: the 3D stage on the left, the step text and sliders on the right. Here the 3D stage fills the whole sheet body and the steps sit in a floating, frosted panel on top of it, like the HUD panels in Klaudiusz's cartography app (`~/Documents/GitHub/cartography-lab-app/apps/web/src/styles/app.css`: `.hud`, `.hud-stack`, `.float-panel`). The sheet stays 960 wide and takes the opening's fixed height (9a D26), so the fridge and the opening share one sheet height. The floating panel becomes a design-system component, documented on the Components page. Scheduled after 9a and before 9b.

## Scope

**In:**

1. The fridge's sheet: 960 wide and the fixed height `clamp(480px, 60vh, 640px)` from 1024px; the sheet's solid header unchanged (D1)
2. The stage fills the sheet body; the panel floats on the right on desktop and is docked at the bottom below 1024px (D2 to D4)
3. A reusable floating panel, `.float-panel`, with frosted glass from the colour tokens, Light and Dark, a solid fallback for reduced transparency, documented with a live example on the Components page (D5 to D7, D13)
4. The camera frames each step's subject in the part of the stage the panel leaves free, on desktop and on a phone; the poses retuned for the new frame (D8, D9)

**Out (not in this branch):**

- The avatar acting (8b)
- Any change to the fridge's steps, copy, sliders, formula, food or the hand-over to the opening (fridge D4, D9 to D12, D23; 9a D1)
- A draggable bottom sheet on the phone (interview: docked, not draggable)
- The cartography app's noise texture on the glass (D6)
- Using the floating panel anywhere else (the opening keeps its plain page); later items may adopt it

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **The sheet** (interview): from 1024px the fridge's sheet is 960 wide (`.modal-shell--wide`) **and** fixed height (`.modal-shell--fixed-height`, `clamp(480px, 60vh, 640px)`, never taller than the viewport allows). `setWide` in `js/fridge-advisor-sheet.js` no longer drops the fixed height for the fridge, so the fridge's 90vh sheet goes. The sheet's header (Back, title, close) stays solid and above the film, exactly as in the opening and the flows, so the film is 960 x about 584 on a tall window. The hand-over to the opening only narrows the sheet (960 to 640); the height no longer moves, and "Start over" only widens it again | Interview. One sheet height across the journey; the chrome does not change at the hand-over. Changes the fridge part of the "Wide flow sheets" and "Fixed-height flow sheets" rows in `tech-spec.md`, which are updated at wrap-up |
| D2 | **The stage fills the sheet body** at every size: the canvas covers the whole body, and the panel floats on it. Replaces the two-column layout of fridge D21 (from 1024px) and the stacked layout of fridge D16 (below 1024px). The page's `html.fridge--wide` switch stays, now choosing between the floating and the docked panel (D4) | The point of 8d. The switch already asks the right viewport (the parent's, `matchMedia` on `window.parent`) |
| D3 | **The panel on desktop** (interview: right): `.float-panel` inset `var(--space-4)` from the top, right and bottom of the body, **anchored at the bottom** so Next stays in one place on every step, its height from its content (it grows upward), at most the body height minus the insets. About 20rem wide, as the cartography HUD. It holds what the right column holds today, in the same order: the step caption, the heading, the text, the fields with their notes, the result ring, the hint and Next. No step scrolls inside the panel at a 640 sheet (9a D27's rule); if step 5 (three sliders) or the result does not fit at 20rem, the panel widens up to 22rem before anything is trimmed (open question 1). **Settled in group 2:** widening does not help (the sliders do not wrap; 22rem saves nothing, 24rem one line), so the panel stays 20rem and its spacing is tightened instead: inset and padding `--space-3`, the panel's gap `--space-1`, the fields' gap `--space-2`, the number fields 2rem high. Step 5 with the hint showing is then 532 of 541px; every other step is lower. On a window too short for the 640 sheet the panel scrolls inside as the fallback | Interview for the side. Bottom anchoring is a default (flagged): Next keeps the place it has in the flows; a top-anchored panel would move Next from step to step |
| D4 | **The panel below 1024px** (interview: docked, not draggable): the panel is docked at the bottom of the full-screen sheet, full width, with rounded top corners and the safe-area inset under Next, its height from its content. The scene fills the screen behind it and the camera frames the subject in the space above it (D8). **Settled in group 2:** the switch is the page's own width, not the parent's viewport: below 640px the panel is docked, from 640px it floats (D3). So a tablet's full-screen sheet (640 to 1023) gets the floating panel too; a docked panel 800 wide stretched Next across the screen and read poorly | Interview for the phone. The tablet range was judged by eye (open question 3) |
| D5 | **The floating panel component** (interview: component plus a Components entry): `.float-panel` in `css/styles.css`, in its own section next to the other shared components, with the modifier `.float-panel--docked` for the bottom-docked form. Built like the cartography panel: the frosted fill and blur on a `::before` layer behind the content, a 1px rim (a light inner highlight on top, a shade at the bottom) and, in Light, a soft drop shadow. Its values are custom properties on the component: `--float-panel-fill` (the background at 70%), `--float-panel-blur` (8px), `--float-panel-highlight`, `--float-panel-shade`, `--float-panel-shadow` | AGENTS.md: shared rules, narrow selectors; mission principle 2: what is new becomes part of the system |
| D6 | **Colours only from the tokens:** the fill is `color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-bg) 70%, transparent)`, the rim and shadow mix `--color-fg`, `--color-bg` and `--color-modal-elevated-shadow` with transparency; no raw `rgba()` as in the cartography app. So the panel follows Light, Dark and every colour theme by itself. No noise texture: the cartography PNG is about 1 MB, and at 8px blur over a 3D scene its grain adds little | AGENTS.md colour rule; no asset weight for a detail |
| D7 | **Fallbacks:** with `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` or `prefers-contrast: more` the panel is solid `--color-bg` with no blur (the rim and shadow stay). Where `backdrop-filter` is not supported, the fill rises to about 92% so text stays readable without the blur. Text on the panel uses the existing `--color-fg` and `--color-fg-secondary`; the camera keeps the subject out from under the panel (D8), so behind the glass is mostly the page background. Contrast is checked by eye in Light, Dark and a Multi-color theme (interview: the standard gate, no measured numbers) | Roadmap 8d's open points (contrast, reduced transparency). If one theme reads poorly, `--float-panel-fill` is the one value to raise |
| D8 | **The free area:** the camera frames each pose in the part of the stage the panel leaves free (left of it on desktop, above it when docked), not in the whole stage. The renderer still covers the whole body; `PerspectiveCamera.setViewOffset` moves the projection centre to the middle of the free area, and the fit (`target()` in `js/fridge-advisor-scene.js`) uses the free area's width and height. The panel's size is read from the page (a `ResizeObserver` on the panel), so a taller panel on a phone step reframes. A reframe after a step change rides on the step's glide (0.8 s, the existing ease); a resize snaps; with reduced motion everything snaps (fridge D15) | Keeps the subject visible and centred in what can be seen; uses three.js's own API, no new code path for the poses. The framing rules of fridge D22 and D24 still hold, measured in the free area |
| D9 | **The poses** in `js/fridge-advisor-config.js` are retuned by eye for the new frames: desktop (about 600 x 550 free) and a 390 x 844 phone with the panel docked (the free area changes with the step). The intro shows the fridge and the persona together; the tour, slider and result steps frame their subject as today. `fovNarrow` stays for a tall free area | The frames change shape; the config is where the numbers live (fridge D7) |
| D10 | **The panel's content and styles:** the step markup does not change (`js/fridge-advisor.js` `render()`); the panel gains the `.float-panel` classes, `.fridge__actions` loses its own `--color-bg` (the panel is the background) and its sticky rule (a sticky row without a background would show the scrolled text through it; see open question 2). The result ring keeps 11rem on desktop and 8.5rem on a phone | Small, contained diff (AGENTS.md); the steps were reviewed in 8a and 8c |
| D11 | **No new motion:** the panel does not slide or fade between steps; its content swaps and its height changes at once, as the right column does today (flagged: an eased height could follow if the jump reads badly). The camera glide, doors, food and the cooling moment are unchanged | Motion has a job (mission principle 4); a moving panel would compete with the camera |
| D12 | **Accessibility:** the order is unchanged: the sheet's header, then the panel (caption, heading, text, fields, Next). Focus rings on the panel use `--color-fg-interactive` as everywhere | Fridge D18 holds; glass changes nothing for assistive tech |

## Context

- The sheet modifiers: `css/styles.css` 2593 to 2606 (`.modal-shell--wide`, `.modal-shell--fixed-height`); `setWide` in `js/fridge-advisor-sheet.js` toggles `--fixed-height` only for the narrow sheet today.
- The fridge's layout: `css/styles.css` from about 11292 (the stacked layout, `.fridge__stage` at 45%, `.fridge__panel`, the sticky `.fridge__actions` with `--color-bg`) and 11502 to 11522 (`html.fridge--wide`: the two-column grid).
- The scene: the renderer is transparent (`alpha: true`, clear colour 0), so the page background shows behind the 3D and the glass blurs both. `target()` (about line 320) backs the camera off until `width` and `height` fit `camera.aspect`; `resize()` (about line 441) sets the size and the narrow field of view from the host element.
- The page: `syncLayout()` in `js/fridge-advisor.js` sets `html.fridge--wide` from the parent's viewport (1024px).
- Measured on 2026-10-29 in a 2391 x 1179 window: the fridge's sheet is 960 x 1061 today (90vh); with D1 it is 960 x 640.
- The reference, cartography `app.css`: `--hud-panel-width: 20rem`, `--glass-fill` (the panel background at about 70%: `#FFFFFFB2` Light, `#080A10B2` Dark), `--glass-blur: 8px`, a highlight and shade rim, a drop shadow of 0 4px 12px at 5% black in Light only, and a noise PNG at 8% opacity.

## Open questions

1. Settled (D3): 20rem with tighter spacing; step 5 fits at a 640 sheet.
2. Settled: at 390 x 844 the docked panel is at most 487px (step 5, hint showing), leaving about 290px of scene above it in a 779px body; nothing scrolls. On shorter phones the panel scrolls inside (`max-height: 100%`); Next is then reached by scrolling the panel, not kept sticky. Whether the scene above it is enough is judged with the camera in group 3.
3. Settled (D4): the panel floats from a 640px page.
