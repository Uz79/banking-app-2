# The risk experience: requirements

Branch: `feature/risk-experience`

Roadmap item 9e (`specs/roadmap.md`). Replaces the presentation of the opening's three standard risk questions (9a D10) with the customer's own amount: a chart of what their money could do over their horizon, in a good, an expected and an unfavourable case, and each question asked in their francs next to it. The scoring stays exactly as it is (9a D12): the same three questions, the same answer ids and scores, the same bands and caps, so the recommendation stays auditable. The reference is the bank's "Warum anlegen?" calculator (`~/Documents/Projects/AI Portfolio Projects/Banking App/_resources/261028_product-opening/scenario/calculator.png`): a fan of three lines over the years with the end values in CHF and a footnote on the range. The interview settled the mapping (one scenario per question, same scores), the chart (a fan over the customer's horizon), the numbers (one balanced mix as an example) and that the plain list goes.

## Scope

**In:**

1. A chart on the risk step: the customer's amount over their horizon, three lines (good, expected, unfavourable) with their end values in CHF, drawn as inline SVG (D1 to D5)
2. The three questions reworded as three scenarios in the customer's francs, each tied to a part of the chart, with the same answer ids and scores (D6 to D9)
3. The chart's example numbers and the horizon's years in the config, flagged as placeholders like the scoring (D3, D4)
4. The step fits the sheet without scrolling on desktop (677 sheet) and at 390 (D11)
5. The assistant's risk chips updated to the new step (D12)

**Out (not in this branch):**

- The scoring itself: the scores, the bands and the caps (9a D12) stay; so does the summary's "Risk profile" row (D10)
- A chart per strategy or a strategy switch on the chart (interview: the strategy is chosen on the next step)
- The plain list of the three questions as an alternative (interview: replaced)
- The persona or the helper reacting to the answers (9f, and 8b's avatar)
- Duration and amount sliders on the chart: the amount and the horizon are answered on their own steps and only shown here (D2)
- Any change to the strategy carousel, the summary's layout or the draft's shape

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **The chart:** one inline SVG at the top of the risk step, no chart library. The x-axis runs from today to the end of the customer's horizon (a few year ticks, "Today" and "In {n} years"), the y-axis is CHF. Three lines start at the customer's amount: good, expected, unfavourable, with the area between good and unfavourable lightly filled. Each line's end value is written as text beside or under the chart: for CHF 50'000 over 5 years "Good CHF 86'700", "Expected CHF 61'100", "Unfavourable CHF 43'000" (with the change in percent, as the reference) *Amended in the build (D11): the end values sit beside the plot on desktop and below it on a phone; no axis labels (the line above says the years).* | Interview: a fan over the horizon, like the bank's "Warum anlegen?". SVG keeps it sharp, themeable and light; `tech-spec.md` has no chart library and this does not need one |
| D2 | **Its inputs:** the amount from the amount step (`answers.amount`, CHF 10'000 as the example if there is none, as the assistant does today) and the horizon from the purpose step. Shown, not editable here: a line above the chart says "Your {amount} over {n} years" | The amount and the horizon are answered on their own steps and are part of the record. Editing them here would change answers behind the customer's back |
| D3 | **The numbers (flagged, placeholders):** one illustrative balanced mix, labelled as an example. Each line is `amount × exp(r × t + z × s × √t)` with `z` 0 for expected, +1.96 for good and −1.96 for unfavourable, so the band holds 95% of outcomes as in the reference's footnote. `r` and `s` live in the config (`RISK_CHART`, first values r 0.04, s 0.08). ## Context

- The risk step today: `risk()` in `apps/web/js/investment-opening.js` (about line 692). One question open at a time; `need()` opens the first unanswered one. `recommendation()` (about line 218) sums `RISK.questions[].options[].score`; nothing there changes.
- `RISK` in `apps/web/js/investment-opening-config.js` (line 78): the three questions with ids, labels, option ids and scores; `SCORING` (line 163) the bands and caps.
- `HORIZON.options` (line 56): `hlt3`, `h3to5`, `h6to10`, `hgt10`. The purpose step comes before the risk step, so a horizon is always there; a draft opened on the risk step without one shows 10 years.
- The assistant gets the customer's amount through `assistant.setValues` (`investment-opening.js` line 136); the risk chips are in `apps/web/js/opening-assistant-config.js` (about line 115).
- `fmt` and `chf` (line 133) format francs with the app's apostrophes; the chart and the options use them, rounded to CHF 100 for the chart's end values.
- The intervention map (`designs/conversation/investment-opening/intervention-map.md`) assigns step 5, Risk, "Form, then Module (9e)". Its row is updated when this is done.
