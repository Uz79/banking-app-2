# Investment opening (9a, the plain flow): requirements

Branch: `feature/investment-opening`

Roadmap item 9a (`specs/roadmap.md`). What follows the fridge's "Show suitable products": the opening of an asset management mandate (five strategies), as a plain form flow with a progress indicator. It is the "before" that the later 9 items improve step by step (the assistant in 9b, the vehicles in 9c and 9d, the risk experience in 9e). Input: the intervention map `designs/conversation/investment-opening/intervention-map.md` and the reference frames of the bank's old flow in `~/Documents/Projects/AI Portfolio Projects/Banking App/_resources/261028_product-opening/scenario` (German). Builds on the fridge advisor (`specs/2026-10-22-fridge-advisor/`, D6 is replaced here, D27 hands the amount over to this spec).

## Scope

**In:**

1. A new page `investment-opening.html` that the fridge sheet loads after "Show suitable products", in the same sheet; the sheet narrows from 960 to 640 (D1 to D4)
2. About ten screens with a step indicator: intro, knowledge, purpose and horizon, amount and the amount check, risk (three questions), strategy, ESG, tax status, documents, summary; then completion, and an ineligibility exit (D5 to D17)
3. The recommended strategy from the risk answers, capped by horizon and knowledge (D12, flagged)
4. A session draft so a closed flow resumes in the same tab (D18, D19)
5. Completion adds an "Asset management" row to Overview, kept with the demo data (D20 to D22)

**Out (not in this branch):**

- The assistant, its chips and the helper character (9b, 9f); no help affordance yet
- Vehicles, the risk experience, personalisation (9c, 9d, 9e, 9g)
- Funding the mandate: a transfer target for the new mandate in the transfer flow (D22; a roadmap item)
- Real documents (no PDFs), real eligibility rules, any backend
- A way into the opening that skips the fridge, apart from resuming a draft (D19)

## Context

- The fridge sheet: `js/fridge-advisor-sheet.js` creates the iframe with `src = 'fridge-advisor.html'` and the title "How much should I invest?" (lines 36 to 60), listens for `uzbank:fridge-state`, `-escape`, `-close` (line 124 onwards), and asks "Leave this guide?" through `UZBankPaymentExitPrompt` when the page is dirty (line 97). The wide shell is `.modal-shell--wide` (`css/styles.css:2579`).
- The fridge's step 7 is `id: 'result'` with `next: 'Show suitable products'` and step 8 `id: 'closing'` (`js/fridge-advisor-config.js:140` to 145); `js/fridge-advisor.js:122` hard-codes `TOTAL = 8` and `done()` (line 284) closes the sheet.
- The step indicator: `js/step-indicator.js`, `.step-indicator` in `css/styles.css:9281` onwards (payment 4 steps, internal transfer 3).
- The payment state keeps only `bookings` and `positions` (`loadState` and `saveState`, `js/payment-state.js:210` to 246); `reset()` (line 554) removes the key. The Overview rows are static HTML (`overview.html` around line 2418); the total is filled by `js/data-render.js:92` from `overviewAccountsTotal`.
- The reference frames show the old flow: an intro promising 10 minutes with four topics, three risk screens, the five strategy cards with risk bar and return stars and a "Recommended" tag, a summary with one "edit answers" button.

## Open questions

- The scoring in D12 is a placeholder for the bank's rules, to confirm at the review stop after strategy.
- Whether a riskier strategy than recommended may ever be chosen in a mandate (D11 takes no; the intervention map leaves it open).
- The width transition of the sheet (D2): whether it reads well while the iframe swaps pages, checked at the first review stop.
