# Phone polish of the fridge and the opening: requirements

Branch: `feature/phone-polish`

Roadmap item 21 (`specs/roadmap.md`), which takes in item 19 (the fridge's result ring on a phone). A pass over the fridge (8a to 8e) and the investment opening (9a to 9d) as a customer meets them on a phone, plus two rules that apply on every width: Next is never disabled, and closing always asks and keeps the progress for the session. Klaudiusz settled points 1 to 6 of the brief before the interview (D1 to D10); the interview settled where the session values live, what happens to them on completion, the prompt's exceptions and how VoiceOver is checked (D11 to D14).

## Scope

**In:**

1. The fridge's sliders at the regular size on a phone (D1)
2. The assistant on a phone as its own layer over the whole flow, the sheet's header included (D2, D3)
3. No disabled call-to-action buttons: Next always enabled, a tap with something missing says what and moves focus there, in the fridge and on every opening screen; the rule recorded in `tech-spec.md` for the whole app (D4 to D6)
4. Why the fridge's "Show suitable products" looks pale, and the fix (D7)
5. The fridge's result on a phone: "You could invest" above the ring, only the amount inside, fitting any amount up to CHF 999'999 (D8, roadmap 19)
6. After a close, focus back on Investment with a visible ring only when the close came from the keyboard (D9)
7. Closing asks on every step of both flows, and the progress is really kept: the fridge keeps its step and values for the session, and Investment reopens where the customer left (D10 to D13)

**Out (not in this branch):**

- Other phone issues outside the fridge and the opening (Profile's character over the sliders at 390, the trade flow's "Continue payment" prompt, Hans Meyer on the Pay schedule)
- Disabled buttons elsewhere in the app: the rule is recorded now and applies to new work; existing disabled call-to-actions outside the two flows are listed in the build (D6) and fixed in their own items (found: Profile's "Reset demo data", roadmap 22)
- The fridge's 3D scene, camera poses and the persona's walk (8d, 8e), apart from what the ring's new layout needs
- The assistant's content, its desktop column and its behaviour by User Type (9f)
- The strategy carousel itself (9d D16 to D20), apart from Next (D5)

## Context

- The sheet: `js/fridge-advisor-sheet.js`. `PROMPTS` (line 31) holds the two prompt texts; `requestClose()` (line 143) asks only when the page reports `dirty`; `resumable()` (line 66) reopens the opening when the draft's `step > 0`; `finish()` (line 108) calls `opener.focus()` unconditionally.
- The fridge: values in memory (`js/fridge-advisor.js:81`, fridge D8), the step in `index` (line 152), the prefill flag `prefilled` (line 86). Sliders are built in `buildField()` with `cc-color-slider cc-color-slider--compact` (line 197). Next is disabled on step 5 by `gate()` (line 351) with the hint above it. The ring and its text are built in `buildResult()` (line 278); the phone rule is the `max-width: 480px` block after `.fridge__year` in `css/styles.css` (ring 8.5rem, amount `--fs-h5`). `showProducts()` (line 308) overwrites the draft with `step: 0`.
- The opening: `render()` in `js/investment-opening.js` (about line 977) gathers `gated` buttons and disables them while `built.missing()` returns text; the hint sits above the buttons. Each screen returns its `missing` (lines 577 to 832). `publish({ dirty })` (line 1051) decides whether the sheet asks.
- The assistant on a phone: `showFull()` / `hideFull()` in `js/opening-assistant.js` (lines 69 to 89) make the aside a full-screen modal inside the frame; the sheet's header, in the parent page, stays visible and reachable above it.
- The opening has no range sliders; D1 is the fridge only.
